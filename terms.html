Terms of Use

These terms cover one private installation of private-banking, run by one person on their own machine for their own accounts. Nothing here is offered to anyone else, so this is short: what the software is, what it is not, and who carries the risk.

Who may use it

Only the operator, Filip Gustafsson. This is not a product, a service or an offering. There is no sign-up because there is nothing to sign up to. The same single person uses it for both personal and company accounts; the company view is a filter over the same local database, not a separate service for anyone else.

It only reads

Access is requested for balances and transactions only. The software contains no payment functionality of any kind and cannot initiate a transfer, change a standing order, or alter anything at a bank. Its effect on your accounts is limited to reading them.

It is not the bank, and holds no licence

Bank access is provided by Enable Banking Oy, an Account Information Service Provider authorised and supervised by the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority. This software is a client of that service and holds no financial licence itself. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for Handelsbanken, Avanza or Lysa; those names appear only to identify whose data is being displayed.

Consent expires

Bank access exists only while a valid PSD2 consent is in place — at most 180 days here, and less where a bank caps it lower. When it lapses, data simply stops updating. Consent can be withdrawn at the bank at any time. Note that withdrawing consent stops future retrieval; it does not delete data already stored locally.

The figures are informational

Nothing displayed is an account statement. Where a number here disagrees with your bank, your broker or your fund provider, their record is the correct one. Investment holdings in particular are only as current as the last file exported by hand, because PSD2 does not expose securities positions — a holdings figure may be weeks old.

Several numbers are computed, not reported

No institution ever stated these; the software derives them, and each can be wrong:

  • Historical balances are reconstructed by walking transactions backwards from a known balance, so they are only as complete as the transaction history.
  • Returns are a chained Modified Dietz approximation over month-end snapshots, with a further simplification at portfolio level.
  • Categories are assigned by text-matching rules and are frequently wrong until corrected.
  • Internal transfers are matched heuristically by amount, sign and date proximity; a missed match distorts income and spending.

Not advice, and not an accounting record

Nothing produced by this software is financial, investment, tax or legal advice. It is not a bookkeeping system, is not designed to satisfy bokföringslagen, and must not be used as the basis for a tax return or an annual report. Company accounting obligations are unaffected by it.

As is, with no warranty

The software is provided as is, without warranty of any kind — no guarantee of accuracy, completeness, availability or fitness for any purpose. There is no support, no uptime commitment and no maintenance obligation. It may break when a bank changes its API or an export format, and may then simply be abandoned.

Liability

This is free software the operator wrote for himself. To the fullest extent permitted by law, no liability is accepted by anyone for any loss arising from its use — including decisions taken on the strength of a figure it displayed.

Security is the operator's own responsibility

There is no login screen. The database is unencrypted, and the private key that identifies the application to Enable Banking is stored unencrypted because it must be usable unattended. The server binds to 127.0.0.1 and is not exposed to the network by default. Anyone with access to the machine has access to everything in it; protecting that machine is entirely the operator's responsibility.

It may stop at any time

The operator may stop running, modify or delete this software and its data at any moment, without notice, and owes no one an explanation. Nothing runs on a schedule — data is only fetched when a command is run by hand.
